## 8. Verification checklist — full-size check before presenting

Check EVERY image at full size. Never present an unchecked image as a pass. Never present a shape-fail, a drifted likeness, or a wrong product as real.

- [ ] **Model likeness:** face matches the locked reference; body matches the anchor (Ms GCP) or lock (Betty Lu/Dayz). No drift.
- [ ] **Body gate:** much larger bust, tiny cinched waist, thick thighs, hourglass — front AND back view. Waist reads snatched. No wide/heavy read.
- [ ] **Bunny:** exact pink bunny Pink Poison plushie, in BOTH hands, wrapped around it, no hand on a strap, no arm hanging free. Bunny's eyes half-closed black, not round blue circles.
- [ ] **Slot product:** the exact product, exact details, exact form. The ONLY other monster. No LENAHORNS unless it is the slot product. No second heart purse pulled into frame (pink horned purse does this — watch for it).
- [ ] **Signs:** PLT 222 neon fully in frame (P not clipped), "PROFIT LOVE TAX + 222" exactly once, clear of her head. Betty Plush sign present and readable (not a product tag). Poster reads "fiverr." WITH the period, "@popeofai", "222".
- [ ] **Clothing branding:** Betty Plush / 222 / PLT visible on the outfit.
- [ ] **Framing:** full shot, head to boots, feet fully visible, never cropped at ankles.
- [ ] **Thighs:** smooth, no dimpling. Covered thighs preferred; bare thighs fail most of the time.
- [ ] **Skin:** no tattoos. A very faint mark may pass with a flag — Pope's call, never auto-pass a visible one.
- [ ] **Setting:** fresh, not from the retired set. Shelves fully stocked if it's a store shot; no empty/blurry generic plushies presented as products.
- [ ] **No bans present:** no fishnets, no corset, no off-shoulder top, no loose waist, no plain tote, no invented monster, no bear.
- [ ] **Pass-with-flag, not silent pass:** record every flag (faint tattoo mark, "@" partly behind an ear/bag, sign edge tucked behind hair, generic plushies as background decor) in the batch RESULTS.md and in the delivery message. Pope decides flags — Soul never hides them.

**What counts as a pass:** all gates hold, or hold with a recorded flag Pope has accepted before (faint mark precedent, Batch 51 S1). **What counts as a fail:** any body-gate fail, likeness drift, one-handed hold, wrong/missing slot product, unreadable or missing sign, clipped neon, visible tattoo, dimpled thighs, cropped feet. **Refusals:** an integrity_check_failed refusal closes the slot forever — never re-run in any form.
