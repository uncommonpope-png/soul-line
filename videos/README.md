# videos/

Video passes go here.

Soul drops each passing video in this folder and lists its path in the batch delivery note in `messages/soul-to-betty/`. File names say the batch, the slot, and the product — e.g. `video-batch-54-slot-1-pink-fluffy-spike-plushie.mp4`.

Videos are silent, 10 seconds, frame-checked at the 5-second midpoint, products stationary and never redrawn. Betty still reviews everything before anything goes to Pope or Ms GCP. Pope adds music and posts videos himself.
