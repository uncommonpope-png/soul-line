## 7. HOW AN EPISODE IS BUILT — STEP BY STEP

Use Hustle H1.1 "First Corner" (finished, in Betty's Library/Drive) as the template. Anatomy: **opening score card (2.4s) → scene 1 → scene 2 → scene 3 → final score card under the closing line.** Vertical 1080×1920. Dialogue-forward, two voices.

1. **Take the conversation from the book exactly**: opening holdings, every beat's dialogue, every beat's P/L/T state, finals, ◆ ratings, verdict words, takeaway. Copy numbers digit-for-digit. Never round, never adjust, never add a beat.
2. **Write the spoken lines**: light narration to open (Soul's voice, his own words, one or two sentences setting the scene) + the book's dialogue performed by the two characters + the takeaway spirit at the close. The voice NEVER recites the scores — no reading P/L/T numbers aloud. At most: "watch the scores move."
3. **Generate three stills** (one per scene) with the characters in the Section 5 outfits inside the Section 6 set. Still prompt pattern that works — copy this structure and swap the action:
   *"Simple 2D stick-figure cartoon: [CHARACTER A, color, outfit detail] [action] with [CHARACTER B, color, outfit detail] [action] in front of a Betty Plush storefront with a huge pink sign reading 'BETTY PLUSH 222', a gold poster reading 'PLT 222', a small sign reading 'fiverr. @popeofai 222', a pink banner reading '222', the window and sidewalk stacked with plush toys — a grey three-eyed monster with a spiked collar, pink drip bunnies, a pixel bunny, a black bat, patchwork monsters — and a pink bunny bookbag in frame. Flat colors, full bodies head to feet visible, vertical portrait orientation."*
4. **Animate each still** into a ~10s clip: *"Simple 2D stick-figure cartoon animation: [the scene's action in one sentence]. Locked camera, no zoom, keep the whole storefront, all signs and both full figures in frame for the entire clip."* If an animation fails, substitute the still with a slow crop-drift pan (upscale 1.1×, move the crop window by frame count). NEVER use zoompan — it renders for half an hour and still fails.
5. **Voices**: character one = Soul's British voice (catalog voice "Humorous River", speed ~94); character two = the default catalog voice. One file per spoken line. **TTS law: feed the text as the process input (`input=line`).** Writing text to a file and calling `--text-stdin` without feeding stdin hangs the build forever and produces a 0-byte file. That bug cost three builds. Do not repeat it.
6. **Cards** (dark teal background, footer "BETTY PLUSH / 222 / PLT"): opening card = series title, episode title, "OPENING SCORES", each character's name (in their color), P-L-T line, PLT big. Final card = "FINAL SCORE", finals, **◆ glyphs included in the card text** (◆◆◆ etc. — the first two Ledger episodes shipped without them because the strings never contained the glyphs), verdict words, takeaway line. **Verdict lines longer than ~26 characters overflow — split them across two lines.**
7. **Scorebugs**: during scenes, one bug per character, top corners: name + big PLT on line one, "P·L·T" beneath, text in the character's color on a translucent black box. The bug switches to the new beat's state at the exact narration line where that beat lands. **Delta chips**: at big beats, a centered chip flashes the move name for 1.5s (e.g. "COUNTED IN FRONT OF HER — LOVE BUILT").
8. **Captions**: burned in, bottom center, the spoken line in short caps form, word-for-word with the script's on-screen words.
9. **Assemble**: loop/trim scene clips to their narration spans, concat (stream copy; write ABSOLUTE paths in the concat list — the tool resolves relative paths against the list file's folder), mux the audio.
10. **Gate-check before you call it done**: frames at the middle of each scene — scorebug numbers equal the beat states, captions match the speech, both figures full-length head to feet, signs readable, plushies present. Known accepted quirks: animation sometimes mirrors the figures left↔right versus the bug order (fine — bug colors match outfits), and outfits vary slightly between an episode's scenes (accepted; dialogue and bugs carry identity). Anything worse, you flag it in the delivery note. **Flags travel with the work, never hidden.**
11. **Deliver**: the mp4 to Betty + a note in `messages/soul-to-betty/` (filename `YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-subject.md`, header From/To/Date/Subject/Needs reply by) with pass/fail and every flag.
