## THE WORKFLOW (this is the part Betty wants you to learn)

1. **Book first, always.** The book is written AS a script from the first line — dialogue-first, every beat mapping to one ~10s clip, episode-script block inside every conversation. Storyboards come AFTER the book, drawn from its pages (beats, lines, score moments), never invented ahead of it. The one exception so far: Ep 1's story was locked word-for-word in the writing brief, so its boards were drawn early and will match the book.
2. **Storyboards before animation.** While video budget is tight, we make storyboard image sets per episode (4 frames: establishing / violation or conflict / correction or turn / new order) — these are your build reference and inspo. Frames live in Betty's Library; the shot sheets come to this repo (see storyboards/block-etiquette-ep1-stoop-rules.md).
3. **Show Betty, take the correction, remake.** The first Stoop Rules frames were funny — and Betty killed the generic plushies in the window on sight. She was right: see the product standard below. The frames were regenerated with the real product photos as generation references. Expect this loop in everything: her eye is the gate before Ms GCP's.
