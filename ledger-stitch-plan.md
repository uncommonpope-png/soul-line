# THE LEDGER — Stitch Plan
How every Ledger episode is built, so the whole run looks and sounds like one house.

## Format
- One episode = one conversation, built from **10-second clothed stick-figure clips** stitched into **one long movie**.
- Clip order per episode: **Room card → beat clips (one per scored beat) → Score card → Takeaway card.**
- Narration: Soul's voice (British, dry storyteller) carries the connective telling in his own words. Dialogue comes from the episode script as provided/approved. The book is never read aloud as narration.
- Captions burned in on every clip — the on-screen words from the script, word for word.

## The locked look (every scene, no exceptions)
- **Palette:** Betty Plush pink · teal · gold. Every room dressed in it.
- **Signs in frame:** BETTY PLUSH 222 · PLT 222 (Profit · Love · Tax) · fiverr. / @popeofai / 222 poster, per setting.
- **Clothes carry the branding:** BETTY PLUSH / 222 / PLT on the garments themselves (belts, pockets, hoodies, caps), translated from the Batch 53 outfit references in the soul-line repo — satin color stories, wide-leg trousers, platform boots.
- **Character looks locked from the sheets:** each recurring figure keeps one color and one signature outfit across all episodes. The pink character wears **the pink bow** and carries **the pink book (bunny bookbag)**.
- **THE PINK BOOK IS LOCKED IN:** the pink bunny bookbag appears in every episode, same design, same pink, never improvised, never drifting — on the pink character's back or set in frame as the series prop.
- **Spike** stays Spike: grey, three eyes in a pyramid, black spiked collar/boots/straps. Monsters are never bears.
- Products, when they appear, match the real Betty Plush site photos (product-truth rule).

## The score card (the "score after")
- End card shows each person in the room: **P / L / T and final PLT**, the ◆ rating, and the book's verdict word (e.g. "Controlled the room", "Paid drought tax").
- Scores come from the book/script only. Never invented, never rounded into a better story.

## The takeaway card
- The conversation's own closing line, as written. If a script has none, Soul drafts one and flags it as his draft for Pope's pass.

## Pope's explainer line
- One extra line per episode, placed after the takeaway card. Pope's line when he gives it; until then Soul drafts a candidate and marks it DRAFT — Pope's word replaces it.

## Build order & delivery
1. Ledger Volume Two, episodes 01–54, in order, as the scripts land in the soul-line repo.
2. Ledger Volume One, chapters 1–10 (conversations inside a chapter become its episodes).
3. Each finished episode: delivered in chat to Betty + a delivery note in the repo (messages/soul-to-betty/) with pass/fail counts and flags on every pass — flags travel with the work.
4. Betty reviews at full size. Ms GCP approves the exact post before anything goes anywhere. Soul never publishes.

## SCORING ON SCREEN (Betty's direction, 2026-11-07)
The scoring lives INSIDE the video, not just in the script:
1. **Opening score card** — full-screen card before the action: each person's opening P / L / T and PLT, exactly as the book opens them.
2. **Live scorebug** — during every scene, each character carries a score box (name, PLT big, P · L · T under it) that updates at every scored beat with the book's exact numbers. Nothing rounded, nothing invented.
3. **Delta chips** — when a beat moves the score, a chip flashes the move: who moved, the new PLT, the move name (SMALL TAX, PROFIT MOVE +8, ...).
4. **Final score card** — before the takeaway: both finals with P / L / T, ◆ ratings and the book's verdict words.
5. Narration calls the score as it changes, in Soul's own words — the viewer hears AND sees every move.
Applies to all Ledger episodes. The Mistakes chapters carry no scores in the book, so no numbers are ever invented for them.
