## Narration (~132 words)
Priya is leaving, and HR wants to know why — the exit interview, the last transaction in a work relationship. Shannon doesn't open with a form. She opens with a promise: I want this to be a real conversation, not just a form. What actually happened? And Priya tells the truth — she stopped feeling like her growth mattered to anyone here. She names the moment: passed over, a promise to "look at a role" six months ago, nothing happened. Shannon doesn't deflect. She asks for specifics, writes it down, and says the feedback is going to matter. An exit interview is only valuable when the person leaving trusts that honesty won't cost them their reference. Shannon built that trust in her first line — and Priya left with her dignity intact.
