## Narration (~137 words)
The show is going great — and then it isn't. One drunk voice from the dark: that's not how it works! The room goes cold. The comedian has a choice: fight the heckler, or play to the room. He does neither of the obvious things. He uses the heckler as material — thank you, sir, we were all wondering about your expertise — and the audience laughs, but the heckler keeps going. So the comedian drops the joke and says it straight: I've got this. You came to watch. Let me give you something worth watching. His Love jumps twelve points in that one line. The heckler sits down. The show resumes. Here's the lesson: the audience is always the real conversation partner. Play to them, not the heckler — and the room comes back with you.
