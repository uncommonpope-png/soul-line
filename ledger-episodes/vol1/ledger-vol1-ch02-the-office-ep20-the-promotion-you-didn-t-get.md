## Narration (~146 words)
James finds out his colleague got the promotion from a company-wide email. Not from his manager. Not from a conversation. An email. So he walks in, calm, and asks for one thing: help me understand. His manager admits she meant to call — a lie that costs more than silence would have — and explains the other man had specific experience. James doesn't argue tenure. He asks the question that turns a loss into a roadmap: what do I need to develop to be the answer next time? Watch the scores: that one question recovers twelve profit points and ten love points in a single sentence. Disappointment is a test of discipline. Don't storm out, don't sulk — ask for the map, then go build the skills on it. And the next time that email goes out, make sure your name is the one on it.
