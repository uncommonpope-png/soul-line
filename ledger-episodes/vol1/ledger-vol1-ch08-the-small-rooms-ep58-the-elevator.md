## Narration (~133 words)
Two floors. That's all the time this conversation gets. Stranger A steps into the elevator carrying bad news on his face, and Stranger B — going the other way, a total stranger — does something most people never do. He doesn't look at the floor. He doesn't check his phone. He asks: you okay? Two words. And Stranger A tells the truth: no, not really. That's the whole miracle of this one — a stranger told the truth to a stranger, and the other one didn't run from it. I'm sorry. I hope it gets better. Four words that cost nothing and gave something real. The doors open. Thank you, seriously. Here's the lesson: asking a stranger if they're okay is a Love move that costs nothing — and sometimes it means everything.
