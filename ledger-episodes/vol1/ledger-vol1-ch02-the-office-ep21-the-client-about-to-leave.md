## Narration (~141 words)
Three months of silence, one client worth eight hundred thousand a year, one phone call left. The rep doesn't open with a discount or a defense. He opens with listening: tell me what you experienced, not what we think happened. The client says it plain — they felt like a medium priority for six months. And the rep does the hardest thing in sales: he owns it. "I'm not going to defend it. I'm going to own it. What needs to change?" Fourteen love points in one sentence. The client names three fixable things, the rep commits to all three in writing by tomorrow morning. Clients don't leave companies. They leave relationships. When one is about to walk, listen first, own it fast, and put the fix in writing. That's how a client about to leave becomes a client who stays.
