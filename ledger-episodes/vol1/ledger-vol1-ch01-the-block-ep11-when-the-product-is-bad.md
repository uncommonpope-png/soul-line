## Narration (~140 words)
Vick sold something he shouldn't have, and Omar is standing in front of him about it — calm, controlled, no shouting. "Vick. What you sold me wasn't right." And Vick stalls with the worst possible answer: "What do you mean, wasn't right?" He already knows what Omar means. That stall costs him eight Love points and buys him nothing. Then Omar brings the leverage: "My people said it was stepped on heavy." Witnesses, without naming names. Now it's not just a bad product — it's a reputation problem. Vick finally does the right thing, late: "Look, I'll make it right. Come back tomorrow." Committing before the argument escalates — that's a Profit move, and it's still recoverable. But only if tomorrow actually happens. Omar's last line is the condition: "This can't happen again." Vick carries that debt until he delivers.
