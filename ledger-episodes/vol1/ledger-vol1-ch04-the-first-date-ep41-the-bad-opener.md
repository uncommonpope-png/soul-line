## Narration (~144 words)
Twenty minutes. That's how long Alex talks about himself before Jessica has said forty words. Dinner, first date, and he's filling every silence with his own story because he's nervous and he doesn't know what else to do. Then she stops him — gently, but she stops him. Do you want to know anything about me? And here's the thing: he doesn't argue. He doesn't double down. He apologizes once, honestly, and then he asks the question he should have asked at the start — what pulled her into teaching? One real question, and the whole table changes. She lights up. She tells him about her fifth-grade teacher. The date is saved. The lesson isn't 'be perfect.' It's this: when you catch yourself talking too much, stop, own it, and ask one specific question. A conversation that recovers beats one that never needed saving.
