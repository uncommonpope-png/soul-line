## Narration (~147 words)
Chen made an error in the quarterly report, and everyone is about to see it. So he walks to the director's office before the director comes to him. He doesn't bury it, doesn't blame the spreadsheet, doesn't wait. He names the error, names the impact — three percent on Q3 — and he's already holding the corrected version. Sent, done, plus a double-check process so it never happens again. Watch what happens to his score: he starts at thirty-four and ends at seventy point eight. The mistake didn't define him. The recovery did. When you get caught making a mistake — or better, before you get caught — come forward first, bring the fix with you, and build the system that stops it happening twice. That's how a thirty-four becomes a seventy point eight — not by hiding the mistake, but by outrunning it with the recovery.
