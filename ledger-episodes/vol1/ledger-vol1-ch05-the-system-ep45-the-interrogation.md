## Narration (~132 words)
Andre didn't do it. But he's sitting across from a detective who has already decided he did, and every word in this room can cost him his freedom. The detective leans in: you were in the area that night, don't make this harder. Now watch Andre's move. He doesn't deny what can be proven. He says yes, he was there — and then he names his alibi: his cousin, her street, her name, her number, straight from memory, no hesitation. That number changes everything. A man with an excuse stammers. A man with a real alibi gives you the phone number. Here's the lesson: in a room where the power isn't equal, never deny something they can confirm. Confirm it yourself, name your proof, and hand it over before they ask twice.
