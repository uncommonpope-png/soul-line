## Narration (~145 words)
He almost didn't make the call. The mentor almost didn't answer. That's how close this conversation came to never happening — and it's the conversation that changed everything. The student reaches out to the one person he thinks might have seen this before, and he tells the truth: he doesn't know if he's on the right path anymore. The mentor doesn't hand him an answer. He makes it safe to tell the truth — not what you should do, what you're actually looking at — and then he gives him the line the whole call was for: right paths feel uncertain the whole time. You're in the right kind of doubt. The student's Love hits ninety-four. Here's the lesson: the conversation that changes everything almost never announces itself. It looks like an ordinary call, on an ordinary day, that you almost didn't make. Make it.
