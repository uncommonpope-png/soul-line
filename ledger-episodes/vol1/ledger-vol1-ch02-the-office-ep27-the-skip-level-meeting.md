## Narration (~138 words)

Taylor walks in having already done the work that matters — she's been building systems that save her team four hours a week, beyond her job description, and she can say so in one sentence, with a number. The EVP isn't there to warn her. He's there to offer her a brand-new team lead role. And Taylor doesn't just say yes — she's already been thinking about what that role would need to look like to work. Her score jumps from fifty-two to eighty-eight point eight, the highest in the chapter. Be ready for the conversation you don't know is coming. Do the work before anyone asks, and have a vision before anyone offers.
