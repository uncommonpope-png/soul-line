## Narration (~143 words)
Two years. That's how long his sister has been carrying this conversation before she finally calls the family meeting. Her brother knows why he's there — he's been waiting to be called in. And she opens the only right way: we love you, before anything else. Then she asks the question that changes the room: what do you need? Not 'you need to get help' — what do you need? And he tells the truth. I don't know how to start. She doesn't panic. She doesn't lecture. She says that's okay, that's enough — we know how to start. You just have to let us. Both Love scores hit eighty-six at the same moment. Here's the lesson: the person who needs help doesn't have to know what to do. They only have to let the people who love them take the first step.
