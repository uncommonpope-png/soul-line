## Narration (~133 words)
Three incidents in thirty days, and two crews are one bad night away from something nobody comes back from. So they sit down. The leader opens with specifics — dates, places, numbers — and the rep answers with something rare: a partial admission. The first two were ours. The third was a response to yours. Not a full confession, not a denial — just enough truth to make the room real. Then the leader makes the move that seals it: if it's confirmed on my end, it gets handled. I need thirty days of nothing. Can you commit? Thirty days. You have it. Handshake. Here's the lesson: in a territorial negotiation, accountability without full admission beats full denial every time. A partial truth you can check is worth more than a perfect lie.
