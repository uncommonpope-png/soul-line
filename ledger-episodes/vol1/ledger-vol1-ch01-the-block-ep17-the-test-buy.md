## Narration (~150 words)
He says he's a buyer. He's actually evaluating. Ricky walks in smooth — "Let me get a fifty. Heard you move the best thing out here." — and Snow, who's done this long enough to spot a test buy, cuts straight through it: "Who sent you? People don't just show up saying they heard things." That's the gatekeeping question, and it drops Ricky's Profit four points on the spot. Now Ricky has a choice: lie, or tell the truth. He tells it. "My man Tommy from the Sixth wants to know if you're the real deal." One honest answer, and the whole conversation pivots. Snow knows Tommy. The test becomes a relationship. "Tell him he can come himself next time." Honesty in a gatekeeping situation is always the highest P. L. T. move — because a lie would have been caught anyway, and the truth just bought Ricky the door.
