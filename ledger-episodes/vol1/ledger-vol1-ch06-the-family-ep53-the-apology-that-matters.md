## Narration (~136 words)
Three years. That's how long James has carried an apology he should have given the day of the fight — about the money, about what he said when he was angry. He finally goes home and says it, plainly: I was wrong. You didn't deserve the way I spoke to you. His father could just accept it and move on. But he does something rarer. He meets the apology with his own accountability — I wasn't clean in that conversation either. We both lost our way. And in that moment the whole thing completes. The father's Love hits ninety-two, one of the highest scores in the entire book. Here's the lesson: an apology isn't finished when it's accepted. It's finished when it's answered with honesty on both sides. That's how two people find each other again.
