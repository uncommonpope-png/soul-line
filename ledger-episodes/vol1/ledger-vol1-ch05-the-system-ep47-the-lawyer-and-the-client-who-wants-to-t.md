## Narration (~130 words)
The arraignment is in one hour, and the client cannot stop explaining. He was there, but he wasn't — he was nearby, he wasn't actually in the — and every half-sentence is digging his own hole deeper. His lawyer cuts him off. Stop. Don't say another word about what happened. That's my job, not yours. He tries again — but I need you to understand — and she says the line that changes everything: she understands more when he doesn't talk. Every word he says is a word she has to fight, not a word she can fix. He finally gets it. He stops. He listens. Here's the lesson: in a legal situation, silence is almost always the highest move you've got. Your lawyer can't unhear what you already said.
