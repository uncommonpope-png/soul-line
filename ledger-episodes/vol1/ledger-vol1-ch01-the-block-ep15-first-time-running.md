## Narration (~146 words)

He tests him with the most important question a mentor can ask: "Being ready means knowing you don't know everything yet. Can you say that?" Jay's Profit dips. His Tax climbs. And then — the pause. That pause before the answer is the whole lesson. "Yeah. I can say that. I don't know everything." Real wisdom, demonstrated, not claimed. Stone hears it and hands him the rest: "Good. Now you're ready. Call me if anything feels wrong. Anything." That safety line is the Love commitment that makes the responsibility safe. A legacy being built in real time. The pause before "yeah, I can say that" is worth more than any answer Jay could have given.
