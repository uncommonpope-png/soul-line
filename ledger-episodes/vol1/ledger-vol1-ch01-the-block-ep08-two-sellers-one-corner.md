## Narration (~145 words)
He set up on the wrong block, and this could have ended in violence. Instead it ends in a referral. Pharaoh has held this corner four years, and Shadow moved in last week. Pharaoh doesn't shout. He doesn't threaten. He says three words: "Walk with me." That's authority before the conversation even starts. Then he does something most people never learn — he names the connection. "I know your cousin. Out of respect for him, I'm telling you this once: this block is mine." Dignity, not a threat. And Shadow answers the right way: "I hear you. Where should I go?" That question turns a territorial dispute into a professional conversation. So Pharaoh sends him to Clement Avenue — and tells him to say Pharaoh sent him. The block is maintained. The network grows. The referral is the move that separates masters from everyone else.
