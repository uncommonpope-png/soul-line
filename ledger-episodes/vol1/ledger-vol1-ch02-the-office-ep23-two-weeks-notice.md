## Narration (~140 words)
Priya is leaving for a competitor, and she tells her manager face to face, direct and professional, before the rumor mill does it for her. The manager's first instinct is the accusation — "a competitor?" — and for a second the whole thing could go sour. But Priya doesn't apologize for her decision, and her manager doesn't pretend to be happy. That's honesty, not hostility. Then Priya does the thing that turns a departure into a reference: she's already started the transition document. Two weeks, smooth, handed over clean. How you leave a job is the last impression that lasts the longest. Leave like the person you want them to remember — direct, gracious, and with the handover already started. The last two weeks are the reference they'll actually give, so make them the best two weeks of the job.
