## Narration (~141 words)
Twenty-three years old, and this one conversation decides the next five years of his life. The offer on the table: three years of probation, no jail time, if he pleads guilty. Go to trial and lose, and it's eighteen months inside, minimum. He asks his lawyer what he should do — and she won't decide for him. What she does is harder and better. She lays out the truth plainly: the evidence is significant, the risk is real, this offer is better than most. Then she hands him the frame that makes the choice his: which consequence can you live with? Probation on your record, or eighteen months inside? Here's the lesson: the best legal advice doesn't make the decision for you. It gives you the truth clearly enough that you can make your own choice — and live with it.
