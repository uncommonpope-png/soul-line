## Narration (~133 words)
Two minutes late, one apology, and a coffee date that could have gone nowhere. Marcus sits down across from Diana, and he does the thing most guys never do — he actually listens. She mentions her block back in Crown Heights, and the saxophone player who sets up outside every weekend. Now watch the move. He doesn't ask where she grew up. He doesn't ask what she does. He asks about the saxophone player. What does he play? One specific question, about one specific thing she just offered him, and the whole room changes. Her smile opens up. The conversation stops being an interview and starts being a date. That's the lesson. Don't ask the question on your list. Ask about the thing they just handed you. Specific beats generic every single time.
