## Narration (~149 words)
Third date. Ryan has caught feelings. Claire hasn't — not yet — and she can feel the gap between them widening at the table. So she does the brave thing. She tells him straight: she enjoys his company, but the romantic spark isn't there yet, and she'd rather say it than let him figure it out from silence. Now watch Ryan. He doesn't argue. He doesn't beg. He doesn't pretend he didn't hear it. He thanks her for the honesty, wishes her well, and walks out of that conversation with his dignity intact. And here's what the numbers say: his Love score finished ten points higher than it started — in a conversation where he got turned down. That's the lesson. You can't control how someone feels about you. You can control how you meet the truth. Meet it with dignity, and even a no leaves you standing taller.
