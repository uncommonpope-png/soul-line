## Narration (~145 words)
You ever walk up to a corner and give yourself away before you even open your mouth? That's Malik. First time buying, trying to look like he's done it a hundred times. Rome has read a thousand new faces, and he clocks three tells before Malik says a word: the pause, the dropped eyes, the nerves. Then Malik says it — "Just a dub. Maybe two." Maybe two. That one little word tells Rome everything: this kid is already thinking bigger, and he will overpay. Rome names the price, two for fifty-five, and it sounds like a deal. It isn't. Malik never negotiates once. He walks off paying new-customer prices on a market he doesn't know yet. That's tuition. Next time, he'll know the real price — but only if he learns the rule first: know what your words reveal before they leave your mouth.
