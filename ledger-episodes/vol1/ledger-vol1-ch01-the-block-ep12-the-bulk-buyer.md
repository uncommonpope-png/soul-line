## Narration (~139 words)
Dante wants to move weight, and he came recommended — but a referral opens the door, it doesn't open the vault. Pharaoh doesn't ask about the product first. He asks about the connection: "Whoever sent you, what did they say about me?" Verification before business. Dante answers clean — Marcus from Southside said Pharaoh moves right — and the credibility lands. Then Dante makes his ask, straight business language: consistent supply, volume pricing. And Pharaoh says yes with conditions: "I need to see how a small move goes first. Everybody starts the same." That's fair, and it's consistent. Dante doesn't argue. "That's fair. I respect that. When can we move?" That acceptance builds more credibility than any push for bulk pricing ever would. The fastest path to bulk pricing is never arguing for bulk pricing on the first meeting.
