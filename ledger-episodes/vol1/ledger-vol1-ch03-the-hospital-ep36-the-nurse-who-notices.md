## Narration (~147 words)
Room four-twelve. The chart is normal, vitals stable — but Nurse Bell has been watching this patient for an hour, and his breathing has changed. Twenty-two years on the floor, and she trusts what she's seeing over what the paper says. The young resident pushes back: the chart is normal. Bell draws the line with precision: the chart is what was. I'm telling you what is right now. Twenty-two years on the floor taught her what no chart can: the patient in front of you is the only data that matters. He goes to check — and that's the right move, the one that saves the patient the chart missed. Experienced nurses save lives that charts miss. If you're the one who's been watching, speak up — and if someone brings you an observation with a timeline, go look. The chart is history. The patient is now.
