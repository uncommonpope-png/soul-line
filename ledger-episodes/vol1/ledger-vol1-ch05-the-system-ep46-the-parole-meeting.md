## Narration (~133 words)
Three years in, and Marcus gets one meeting, one shot, with an officer who has heard every story there is. He doesn't walk in hoping. He walks in ready. A job lined up at his uncle's shop — twenty-two years running — starting the week he gets out, and he's holding the letter to prove it. Housing? His mother's house, the one he grew up in, address and phone number ready before she's even asked. The officer doesn't have to chase a single fact. Approval comes before Marcus even leaves the room. Here's the lesson: in a room where the power isn't equal, preparation is the only move you've got. Win the conversation before you walk in — letter, address, number — and the meeting is just the paperwork. That's the whole job.
