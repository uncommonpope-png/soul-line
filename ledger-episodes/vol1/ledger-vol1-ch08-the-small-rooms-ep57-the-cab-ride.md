## Narration (~148 words)
Late night, long day, twenty-two minutes in the back of a cab — and the passenger is trying to be human anyway. The driver opens soft: long night? And the passenger does the one thing that turns a silent ride into a story. He notices the driver. You too, looks like. That one line — seeing the person doing the driving — opens the whole conversation. The driver tells him about twelve-hour days, about meeting everyone in this job. The passenger asks the best question of the night: what's the most interesting ride you've had? And the driver tells him about a woman who went to her own funeral — not dead, just curious who'd show up. Both Love scores finish above eighty. Here's the lesson: notice the person in front of you. One small act of seeing turns a stranger into a story you'll both keep telling.
