## Narration (~148 words)
Somebody said Mello's name where it shouldn't be, and now the rumor is running. Spence heard it — and instead of spreading it, he walks straight up to Mello and asks him direct: "I heard your name in a conversation it shouldn't be in. I'm asking you straight." That walk is the move. Coming directly instead of letting the rumor run is the highest Love move in this chapter, because rumors are expensive whether or not they're true — Mello's Tax is already at sixty-two before he says a word. But Mello doesn't panic. He asks for the source, he stays steady, and then he answers with energy: "No. And whoever said it needs to check what they think they know." Twelve Profit points in one line. Tax drops eight. A direct denial, no hedging. Spence did his job. Mello defended himself. And the rumor dies right there.
