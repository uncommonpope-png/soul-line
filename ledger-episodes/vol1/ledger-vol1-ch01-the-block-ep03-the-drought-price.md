## Narration (~150 words)
Supply is down, and Reggie needs product bad. But here's the rule: never let need show before you know the price. Reggie opens smart — "What you working with?" — neutral, no desperation. Then Santos says it flat: prices are up, everything doubled. And Reggie breaks. "Come on man, that's too much. Can't you work with me?" That's a plea, not a negotiation, and Santos hears it. Three people waiting, drought prices or nothing. Reggie's Profit bleeds with every line, his Tax climbs to forty-eight, and he ends up taking whatever he can afford. The drought didn't beat him. Showing his need did. When you're the only one with supply, need is the most expensive thing in the room. So next time you're buying in a drought, keep your face still, keep your need quiet, and never let them smell it — because the price doubles the second they do.
