## Narration (~136 words)
A parking lot. Two men, both already calculating, both Tax scores already high before a word is said. Devon throws the challenge out there — so what are you going to do? — and Marcus does something most people can't do with their pride on the line. He thinks out loud. He says he's trying to figure out if this is worth it. And then he answers his own question: it's not worth it. Four words, and both Tax scores drop six points in a single sentence. The fight that was about to happen just... doesn't. Devon lets it go. They both walk away. Here's the lesson: the Profit of walking away is the only number in a fight that keeps rising. Everything else — your pride, your reputation, your night — only ever goes down.
