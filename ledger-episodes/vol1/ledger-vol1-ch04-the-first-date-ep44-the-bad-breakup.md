## Narration (~140 words)
Derek knows it's over. But instead of saying it clean, he hides behind the oldest line in the book — it's not you, it's me — and every cliché costs him. Maya doesn't yell. She doesn't beg. She asks him one straight question: just tell me what actually happened. And when he finally does, when he stops hedging and says he doesn't feel the same way, she does the most powerful thing in the room. She thanks him for saying it to her face. She walks out with her dignity. Look at the ending: her Love finished six points higher than his — and she was the one being broken up with. That's the lesson. You don't win a breakup by making it hurt less. You win it by telling the truth plainly, and by receiving the truth with grace.
