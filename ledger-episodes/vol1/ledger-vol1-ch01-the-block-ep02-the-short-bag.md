## Narration (~148 words)
Your regular walks up at eleven at night and says the bag was short. What do you say first? Darius says the worst thing: "Nah, it wasn't short. I weighed that myself." He hasn't even asked what Tone saw. That denial costs him ten Love points on the spot, then fourteen more when he makes it about his reputation instead of Tone's experience. Tone stays calm, stays specific — a gram, maybe a gram and a half — and keeps the whole thing about one transaction, one night, one man. And then Darius finally asks the right question: "How short you think it was?" One question. Six lines late, but it changes everything. He stops defending and starts fixing. "Come back tomorrow, I'll make it right." That one sentence rebuilds twenty-four Love points in a single move. A handled complaint builds more trust than a transaction never questioned.
