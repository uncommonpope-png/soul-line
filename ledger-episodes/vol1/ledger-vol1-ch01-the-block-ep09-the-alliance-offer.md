## Narration (~141 words)
Two operations that could help each other — if they can get past the suspicion. Stone makes the first move: "I think we can both eat better together than separately." Not subordination, collaboration. Vega doesn't commit, but he doesn't shut it down either: "Tell me what that looks like." Stone lays it out clean — east and west, same supply, split the cost, better margin. Then Vega asks the hard question, the one that kills most partnerships before they start: "What happens when our customers overlap?" And Stone does the thing that changes everything. He doesn't fake it. "I don't have a perfect answer, but I'm not pretending I do." Both Love scores jump six points in one line. That's the shift — from negotiation to partnership. Honesty about what you don't know builds more trust than manufactured confidence ever will.
