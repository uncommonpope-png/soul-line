## Narration (~132 words)
A family is split over a do-not-resuscitate decision. The son believes in fighting; his sister wants comfort care. The palliative doctor's job isn't to pick a side — it's to help the family find each other. The son opens with the word that frames everything: giving up. The doctor reframes it in one sentence: no one in this room is giving up on your father. This conversation is about what he wanted. Then she asks the question that unlocks the whole thing: did he ever say anything about how he wanted to live? And the son remembers — he said he never wanted to be a burden. He said that a lot. The family already had the answer. The best conversations don't create consensus. They help people find what was already there.
