## Narration (~139 words)
Something about this customer isn't right, and Kenny feels it before a word of business is spoken. Davis walks up smooth: "Yo, what's the word out here?" Wrong. Nobody who lives here talks like that. Kenny's Tax spikes on the spot. Then Davis drops a name — "Big Lou sent me" — borrowing credibility he can't prove. Kenny doesn't accuse him. He doesn't confirm anything. He just hands Davis a door he can't walk through: "Lou know my number. Tell him hit me." That's the exit move, and it's worth more than any entry move you'll ever make. No scene, no argument, no proof offered or demanded. The undercover can't complete the one verification step that matters, so the whole thing shuts down clean. Remember: you don't have to win the conversation. You just have to leave it right.
