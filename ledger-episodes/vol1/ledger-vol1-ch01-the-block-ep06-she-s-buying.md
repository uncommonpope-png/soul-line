## Narration (~132 words)
A woman walks up to the corner for the first time, and Ty greets her with the worst possible opener: "You lost?" One question, and he's already spent Love on both sides before a single dollar is mentioned. Keonna doesn't flinch. She's calm, she's direct, and she tells him exactly why she's here — a twenty, for a friend who sent her. No drama, no apology, just a clean transaction. And Ty does the one thing that saves him: "My bad." Two words, no over-explaining, and both scores start climbing again. Keonna doesn't hold it against him, and they finish the deal above where they started. The lesson is simple and it costs nothing: lead without assumptions. If you'd never said the dumb thing, you'd never have needed to recover from it.
