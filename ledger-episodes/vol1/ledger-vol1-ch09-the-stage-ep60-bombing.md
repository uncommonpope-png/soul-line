## Narration (~136 words)
The joke doesn't land. You can feel the room go cold — the audience was with him, and now they're evaluating. Most comics do one of two things here: push through pretending nothing happened, or panic. He does a third thing. He names it. I can tell that one didn't land. Then he pivots — completely different angle — and tells them something real, something that actually happened to him last Tuesday that he still hasn't processed. And the room leans back in. His Love finishes higher than it started, and the audience's Love finishes at eighty-two. Here's the lesson: the Love score is always higher after a handled bomb than after a bit that never needed saving. Acknowledge the miss. Pivot to something real. The room will follow honesty faster than it follows a punchline.
