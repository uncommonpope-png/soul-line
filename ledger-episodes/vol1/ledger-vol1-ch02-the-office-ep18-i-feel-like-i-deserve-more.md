## Narration (~144 words)
Five years on the job, a bigger role than she was hired for, receipts in hand — and Maya opens the most important conversation of her year with three words that hand the whole thing away: "I feel like." Her manager doesn't argue. He compliments her, then asks what she had in mind — and just like that, whatever number she says becomes the ceiling. She hedges: maybe, like, fifteen percent? He counters with four or five, drifts up to seven without being pushed, and she thanks him for it. Less than she asked for. Less than she's worth. Less than he had. Here's the correction: feelings don't negotiate, data does. Walk in with the market range, your current number, and the gap — and say it like it's a fact, because it is. Never thank anyone for paying you less than you're worth.
