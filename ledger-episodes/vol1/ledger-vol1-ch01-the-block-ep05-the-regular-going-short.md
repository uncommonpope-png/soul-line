## Narration (~141 words)
Carl's been coming every Tuesday for eighteen months. Same corner, same routine — "Same as always." And that's the warm-up for the ask: "I'm a little short today. Spot me the difference? I got you Thursday." Second time this month. Dray names it straight: "Carl, you said that last Thursday too." And here's the ledger truth — every credit request is a withdrawal. Carl's Love drops fourteen points from his own mouth, and he never made deposits to match. He keeps saying "I always come through," but the pattern says otherwise, and the pattern is the Tax. It compounds. So Dray does the hard thing and draws the line: "Last time, Carl. After this it's cash only." A boundary costs Love in the moment. No boundary costs the whole relationship. If you're the one always asking, start depositing before you withdraw.
