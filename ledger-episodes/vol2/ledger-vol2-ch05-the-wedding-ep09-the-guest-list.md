## Narration (~147 words)
Two hundred people, Mama. We can't afford two hundred people. The guest list is spread across your mama's kitchen table like a crime scene. She's at the stove, stirring, not looking. Denise is coming, she says. I haven't seen Denise in four years, you say. She's family. That's six plates at eighty dollars a plate. She turns around, spoon in her hand. A wedding is not a dinner party, she says. It's the family saying, we see you. I know, Mama. But every plate is also money we don't have. I'd rather have fifty who love me than two hundred here for the free food. Her face changes — surprised, then slowly proud. Can we cut it to a hundred? Your fifty, my fifty. She puts the spoon down, walks over, and hugs you, the list crinkling between you. A hundred, she says. But Denise is coming.
