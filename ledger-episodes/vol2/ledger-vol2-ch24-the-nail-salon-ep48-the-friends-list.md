## Narration (~150 words)
Nineteen-year-old Brianna works the front desk at her auntie Denise's nail salon for the summer — and three of her school friends sit in the waiting chairs, waiting on free sets. Denise walks in, purse down — she already knows. "Brianna. Who are they?" "They're my friends, Auntie. I was gonna charge them, like, twenty. For the polish." Denise pulls the appointment book over. "Twenty for a set that costs seventy-five. Baby, the rent on this shop is twenty-four hundred a month. Every free set, you pay the other fifty-five out of my pocket." Then the line that lands: "You want to treat your friends, pay the difference yourself. Don't be generous with somebody else's business." Brianna walks to her friends and says it plain so the whole room hears it: "It's seventy-five. I can book you for Thursday." One leaves. One stays and books. The friends list is closed.
