## Narration (~149 words)
Denise walks into Frank Deluca's office with a folder and a look that says she's already made up her mind. She's here to bail out her husband. Again. Third time this year. Frank says it straight: the last two times, you paid, he promised, and here we are. He's my husband, she says. And you're his wallet, Frank says. There's a difference. If you don't bail him out, he sits in there for two weeks. If you do, he sits in your house for two years doing the same thing. Pick your cell, Denise. Cruel would be taking your money without saying it, Frank says. So he says it first. And Denise hears her own life said back to her — and she picks up the folder. She doesn't pay. She walks out. The most expensive bail you'll ever post is the one that buys somebody else's next mistake.
