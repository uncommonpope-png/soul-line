## Narration (139 words)
Three weeks of silence. Three weeks since the final interview, no email, no call, nothing — and Andre is staring at his phone like it owes him money. His friend says move on, they ghosted you, it happens. But Andre doesn't sit and wait to be chosen. He writes one short email: loved meeting the team, no rush at all — and here's one quick idea for the spring campaign, while it's fresh. One page. One idea. Good enough to get forwarded to the boss. Monday morning the phone rings. Come in Thursday, meet the director. The profit didn't come from the interview. It came from the follow-up. Three weeks of wondering was the tax — the cost of a company that can't send a two-line email. Don't wait to be chosen. Follow up with value, not with worry.
