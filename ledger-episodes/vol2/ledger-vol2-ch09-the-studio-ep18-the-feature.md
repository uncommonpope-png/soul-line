## Narration (150 words)
"You want me on your song, but you want me to rap about what?" Maya's in the booth, headphones around her neck, staring at the lyric sheet like it insulted her. It's a love song — the girl leaving. "I don't rap about girls leaving," she says. "I rap about girls leaving MEN. There's a difference, and if you don't know it, you don't know my music." "It's a verse," he says. "Do it." She sets the headphones down. "You called ME because my fans listen. You want my audience — but you want me to shrink to fit your song. That's not a feature. That's a costume." He goes quiet. Then: "So what do you want the verse to say?" "Let me write it. My words, my story, on your beat." He nods. She puts the headphones back on. Don't shrink to fit the song. Write your own verse.
