## Narration (~131 words)
Denise is the first in her family to finish college, and an hour before the caps go in the air, her professor pulls her into a hallway. Dr. Alvarez says what Denise hasn't said out loud yet: you think you got here because you were lucky, or because somebody felt sorry for you. You didn't. You got here because you stayed. Denise almost quit three times. Her mother cleans offices at night and used to bring home the pens people left behind, kept in a jar for school — and Denise couldn't quit on a jar of pens. Staying is the whole degree, the professor says. The rest is paperwork. Denise walks onto that field a little taller than she walked in, holding a diploma she hasn't even looked at yet.
