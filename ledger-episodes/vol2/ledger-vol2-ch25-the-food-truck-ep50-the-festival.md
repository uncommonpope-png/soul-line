## Narration (~148 words)
Vivian runs the summer festival — clipboard, headset, and a smile that's talked a hundred vendors into bad deals. She finds Malik at his truck: "Five thousand people — and the spot is free!" Free — except twenty percent of sales, and a hundred free samples. Malik laughs, but it's not happy. "I pay for the food, the gas, my cousin, ten hours in the heat — then I give you twenty percent and a hundred plates for free? That's robbery with music." "Exposure," she says. "Exposure don't pay my truck note," he says. Last year a deal like this left him less than a Tuesday. So he names his number: two hundred flat, no percentage, no free samples, a one-dollar taste cup instead. She writes it down, he watches her write it. The deal becomes real. One plate on the house for the deal. Not a hundred.
