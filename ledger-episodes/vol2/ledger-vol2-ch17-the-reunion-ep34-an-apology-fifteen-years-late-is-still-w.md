## Narration (~134 words)
Fifteen years. That's how long Andre has carried a grudge against Terrence, his old friend, over a promotion back in 2011 — a job Andre thought was his, a thank-you he never got. They haven't spoken since. Tonight, in the corner of the reunion ballroom, with drinks neither of them is drinking, Andre finally says it plain. And Terrence says the thing Andre never knew: he didn't know Andre went to bat for him. He thought saying thank you meant he hadn't earned it. Then he says it, five seconds late by fifteen years — thank you, I mean it. Andre forgives him on the spot. The grudge cost them both fifteen years. The thank-you cost five seconds. An apology fifteen years late is still worth more than a grudge carried one more day.
