## Narration (~136 words)
She's at the gate, boarding pass in her hand, phone to her ear — and the man on the other end waited three whole weeks to call. Three weeks of silence, and he picks the twenty minutes before her plane leaves. She doesn't yell. She doesn't have to. Quiet costs more. She tells him plain: you didn't mess up, you chose. There's a difference. He says he loves her. She says he loved the idea of her waiting — and that's not the same thing. Then the final boarding call sounds, and she hangs up. Not angry. Finished. Because here's the thing: his delay was his. The minute she let it set her schedule, it became her deadline too. Say it when it's yours to say — or lose the right to say it at all.
