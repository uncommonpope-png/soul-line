## Narration (~150 words)
Third inning, Saturday at the Little League field, and Andre walks up the bleachers with two snow cones — his boy, little Andre, is on the bench for the third game in a row. "Ray, my boy practices hard. He shows up every day. He can play shortstop." Ray takes the snow cone Andre offers first — take what a man offers before you tell him no. "Your boy can field, Andre. But shortstop is knowing where to throw before the ball gets to you. It's talking to the outfield. Your boy's quiet out there. He don't talk yet." Then Ray gives him what a parent wants — not a promise, a plan. "He starts next Saturday. I've been working him at short all week. Tell him tonight — and tell him to spend this week talking on that field. Loud." Andre hands him the second snow cone. "Next Saturday."
