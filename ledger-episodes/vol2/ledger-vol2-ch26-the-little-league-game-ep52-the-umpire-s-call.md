## Narration (~143 words)
Bottom of the fifth, two outs, bases loaded — the umpire calls strike three. Wayne, a daddy three rows up, loses his mind. "That was a ball! Outside by a mile! You blind, man? These are kids!" The umpire doesn't turn around. But Denise, the team mom two seats down, puts a hand on his arm. "Wayne. Sit down." "Did you see that pitch?" "I saw it. But your boy is in that dugout watching his daddy scream at an old man — and he's learning something. You want him to learn that when things don't go your way, you yell? Or that sometimes the call goes against you?" Wayne looks at the dugout — his boy is watching him. He sits down, slow, like a man setting down something heavy. After the game, he apologizes to the umpire, and to his boy.
