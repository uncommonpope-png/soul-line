## Narration (~149 words)
The apartment has big windows, afternoon sun, wood floors soft under your shoes — and a kitchen faucet dripping like a metronome. Ms. Hart, the leasing agent, is already talking: eleven hundred a month, first and last, four-hundred deposit. "It's a steal." Simone nods, but she walks the rooms slow, the way her mama taught her — opens every cabinet, checks under the sink, and finds a bucket under the pipe. "Ms. Hart, what's this bucket for?" "Oh, that's just a small leak." "Before I move in, or after? A small leak in a showing is a big leak by move-in day." She names all three problems — the leak, the faucet, the burner that won't light — and trades a twelve-month lease for a three-hundred deposit, repairs written into the lease, not a work order. Never fall in love with a room before you check under the sink.
