## Narration (~131 words)
The groom's got cold feet forty minutes before the ceremony — that's exactly what best men are for. I can't do this, you say, sitting on the bed in your tux, tie undone. What if I'm not ready? Chris doesn't sit down. He stands at the mirror, fixing his cufflinks, calm as anything. You're not ready, he says. Your daddy threw up twice before he married your mama, he says. Then forty years with her. You love Maya, Chris says. You've loved her since she beat you at spades at the cookout and you pretended you let her win. You did not, he grins. Cold feet means you know it's real, he says. If you didn't care, you wouldn't be scared. The fear is the receipt. You stand up. Let's go.
