## Narration (~132 words)
A woman walks into Ray's garage alone, holding a repair estimate for twenty-two hundred dollars. Another shop told Gloria her transmission is shot, and if she drives it, it'll die on the highway. Ray looks at her ten-year-old Honda, looks at the paper, and tells her the truth instead: the transmission is fine. The fluid is low, one sensor is dirty. Two hundred dollars, parts and labor, out by lunch. She asks why he's telling her — he could have charged a thousand and she'd have thanked him for the discount. His daddy ran this shop forty years, he says. A customer you cheat once is a customer you lose forever, but a customer you save once tells ten people. And Gloria told ten people. Three of them showed up that week.
