## Narration (~133 words)
She's been standing by the punch table for twenty minutes, deciding whether to leave. Monique almost didn't come to her own reunion — she's been telling herself she peaked in high school, and everybody here knows it. Then Vanessa finds her, an old friend from senior year, and says the thing nobody else has said: you didn't peak, you paused. There's a difference. They trade memories — the teacher who failed her and loved her at the same time, the songs they used to scream in the car with the windows down. And when the DJ plays their song, Monique dances like nobody's watching right now. Because the people who knew you before the world got its hands on you are the only ones who can remind you who you were all along.
