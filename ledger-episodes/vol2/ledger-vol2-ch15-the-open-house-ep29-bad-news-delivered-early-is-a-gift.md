## Narration (~149 words)
The house sold three hours before the open house even started. Cash offer, no inspection, a developer who wants to tear it down — and nobody told Carla, the listing agent, until she was standing in the living room with fresh flowers and a sign-in sheet. Then the first couple walks in. Josh and Priya, holding hands, already in love with the kitchen. She can see the kids' homework at that table. She can already see it. Carla has about four seconds to decide. And she decides to say the thing that costs her the commission and keeps her the name people trust: there's already an offer. Cash. The sellers decide tonight. You're not too late, she says. You're just not first. If you want it, we fight for it. Bad news delivered early is a gift. Bad news delivered late is a betrayal with a bow on it.
