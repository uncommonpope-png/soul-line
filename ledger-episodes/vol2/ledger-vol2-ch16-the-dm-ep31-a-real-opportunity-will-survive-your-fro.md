## Narration (~140 words)
Eleven forty-seven at night. A stranger slides into her DMs with the sweetest compliment she's heard all year — he saw her night-market photos, he curates a show in Chicago, he wants to talk. Her thumb hovers over yes. She wants this so bad she can taste it. But instead of answering in the dark, she checks. The gallery is real. The account is nine days old, the followers are bought, and the contact page has no address. So she does the one thing the trap can't survive: she points him to the front door. Send it to my email, she says. It's on my profile. He pushes back — galleries don't do email first. She holds the line. A real chance can wait for daylight and a real address. A trap needs you rushed, secret, and off the record.
