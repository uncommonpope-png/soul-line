## Narration (~145 words)
You ever owe a man so long the tab stops feeling like a tab? Andre walks into Leroy's Corner Mart after his warehouse shift, grabs a sandwich and a drink, and says, "Put it on the book." Three weeks of that, and the book says forty-one dollars. Leroy folds his newspaper slow and lays the notebook open like a Bible. "Forty-one dollars ain't a tab no more, son. That's a loan, and I ain't a bank." Here's the move: Leroy doesn't cut him off and he doesn't let it grow. "Twenty on Friday, you keep buying. Nothing on Friday, the book closes." Andre could've walked out mad. Instead he looks at his own name in somebody else's handwriting and says, "Twenty on Friday. I can do twenty." Leroy gives him the sandwich free. Andre pays cash for the drink. The line held, the customer stayed.
