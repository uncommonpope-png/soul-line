## Narration (142 words)
You walk into the interview knowing your number — and then they ask the question, and you fold. "I'm flexible." That's what Darius said, and the second it left his mouth, he heard how weak it sounded. The manager smiled and offered sixty-five. Sixty-five was a raise. Sixty-five was safe. And sixty-five was fifteen thousand less than the job was worth — Darius had looked it up the night before. So he took a breath and said it plain: "I'm looking for eighty." Then he did the smart part. He didn't build a wall. He opened doors: a signing bonus, a six-month review, extra days off. Name your price, then give them ways to meet you. She came back with seventy-eight and a review in six months. He never would have gotten there from sixty-five. The first number is never the number.
