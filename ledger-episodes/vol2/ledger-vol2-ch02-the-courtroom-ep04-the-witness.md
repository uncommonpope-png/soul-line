## Narration (~139 words)
You're in the witness box, and the defense lawyer stands close. You told the police you saw her client's car that night. About eleven, maybe eleven-thirty. Then, soft: you also told the police, in your first statement, that you were home asleep at eleven. Did you forget that part, or did the story change? The courtroom goes still. Your mouth opens. Closes. You were up, you couldn't sleep, you went to the window — so your first statement was a lie, or this one is? Which is it? The prosecutor objects, but the judge waves him down. Answer the question. You look at the jury. Then at your shoes. I don't remember exactly. That pause right there is the blunder. Not the lie — the pause. Twelve strangers decided in one second they can't trust a word you say.
