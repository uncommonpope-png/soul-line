## Narration (~149 words)
You're under the bar with two-forty-five loaded, and your training partner of four years is scrolling his phone. You gonna spot me, you snap, or just stand there looking pretty? He spotted you last set, he says. You said you had it. I had two-twenty-five, you say. This is two-forty-five. There's a difference. The difference is twenty pounds and your ego, he says. You rack the bar hard. Forget it, you say. I'll ask somebody who actually shows up. He stands up. I've shown up four years, he says. Four years, six a.m., every Tuesday and Thursday. One time I check a text and suddenly I don't show up? The anger drains out, and something embarrassed takes its place. My bad, you say. That was foul. I'm just nervous about the weight. Then say you're nervous, he says. Don't say I don't show up. I got you. Now lift.
