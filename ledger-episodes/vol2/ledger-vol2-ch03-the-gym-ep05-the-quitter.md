## Narration (~147 words)
The treadmill's still running. Nobody's on it. You've got your gym bag on your shoulder and your towel around your neck, already decided, just asking permission to leave. Three months, you tell the coach, and you still hate every minute of it. You hate the workout, he asks, or you hate that the mirror hasn't changed yet? Both, you say. Three months, he says. Most people quit in three weeks. You tripled that. That's not failure. That's the part where it starts working and it feels the worst. You stepped on the scale this morning. Same number. Same exact number. Scales lie, he says. Your jeans don't. When's the last time those jeans fit like that? You look down at yourself, surprised, like you're noticing your own body for the first time in weeks. One more month, you say. But if the scale don't move, I'm out.
