## Narration (144 words)
"So we're just... not getting the deposit back?" Marcus is staring at the empty apartment, boxes everywhere, and his roommate is holding the move-out checklist like it's a court document. The landlord docked two hundred for a stained carpet — a stain that was already there when they moved in. They took pictures. But that's not the real fight. "I paid the deposit," Jay says. "All twelve hundred. You were supposed to pay me back half, and you never did." Silence. The empty apartment echoes. Marcus looks at the floor. "You're right. I owe you six hundred. And I'm sorry it took a move-out checklist for me to say it." He pulls out his phone and sends it right there, before he can talk himself out of it. Say you owe it before the checklist does. The stain was already there. The debt wasn't.
