## Narration (~136 words)
He's known for two days what that message says, and he's been leaving it unread on purpose. It's from Simone, his ex. She's got his grandmother's Bible, found it while moving, and she wants to hand it back in person. Ten minutes, coffee, same place, Saturday noon. He knows ten minutes with Simone has never once been ten minutes — he's already paying for three days of thinking about it before Saturday even arrives. But some doors you don't open for the person on the other side. You open them for what's yours on the other side. Saturday comes. She hands him the Bible, wrapped careful in a paper bag. Nine minutes, no big speeches, just goodbye done right. He walks out holding what his grandmother held, and for the first time, ten minutes is enough.
