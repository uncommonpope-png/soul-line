## Narration (~150 words)
You walk into your barber's shop talking about a ten-dollar cut like it's still 2012, and you hover in the doorway. First mistake. In a barbershop, you sit or you leave. You tell him you've been coming six years, like loyalty is a discount card. He doesn't raise his voice. He drops it. Thirty-five, same as the sign. The shop on Fifth does it for twenty? Then go to Fifth — but Fifth doesn't know your hairline, your mama's name, or how you text at six-forty for a seven o'clock date. That lands. You try thirty. He says thirty-five, because you wouldn't be standing here arguing if you didn't already know he's worth it. So you sit in the chair, you pay the price, and he throws you something free: next one's on him if the date goes good. He held his price and kept you. That's the whole game.
