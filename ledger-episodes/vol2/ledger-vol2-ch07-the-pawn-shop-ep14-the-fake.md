## Narration (149 words)
A customer slaps a watch on the counter and demands the manager. A Rolex — or it wants to be one. Lena picks it up, turns it over, and she already knows. The weight's wrong. And the second hand ticks. Real ones sweep; this one ticks like a gas-station clock. "Sir, this isn't real." He says he paid eight thousand dollars for it. She says it gentle and plain: then somebody took you for eight thousand, and I can't take it for eight hundred. Fifty for the band — the band's real gold. He asks if she's calling him a liar. "I'm calling the watch fake. You might be the victim here. That's different." He takes the fifty, and her note on what to check next time. She lost a sale and kept her shop honest. Check the weight. Watch the second hand. A fake costs more than money.
