## Narration (149 words)
"Rent's going up two hundred dollars." The landlord says it standing in the doorway, not coming inside — like two hundred dollars is a small thing. Tasha's holding her baby on her hip, and her body goes still. Four years, never missed a payment, not once. "Everything went up for me too," she says, steady, though it costs her everything. "Groceries. Daycare. Gas. And my job didn't give me a raise." He offers a hundred — and he'll fix the sink that's been leaking since March. "A hundred," she says. "And the sink. And the back door lock. Broken since last summer." He sighs. "The lock too. That's my final." "Deal." She didn't win. She didn't lose either. She turned a two-hundred hike into a hundred — and two repairs she'd been begging for since March. Trade acceptance for repairs. A good tenant is worth more than a hike.
