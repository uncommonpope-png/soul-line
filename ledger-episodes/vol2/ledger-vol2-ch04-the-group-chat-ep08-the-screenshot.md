## Narration (~142 words)
Why is my business in the group chat? Her text lands at 1:47 a.m. Just that. The chat goes dead silent. You screenshotted my message, she writes, and sent it to the group. The one I sent you in private. You take nine minutes to reply — a lifetime at 1:47 a.m. I didn't think it was that deep, you type. You didn't think, her voice note says. I told you that in confidence. I told you because you're my sister. Screenshots don't fade. A spoken word dies in the air. A screenshot lives forever, timestamp and all. Your place in the group — the funny one everybody tells things — just took a beating no apology text can undo. I said I'm sorry, you type. I don't tell you things anymore, she says. That's what you bought. Not a fight. Distance.
