## Narration (~131 words)
The phone rings at 6:12 in the morning, and Andre picks up on the second ring because 6:12 calls are never good news. It's his sister Lena, crying. Their mama is gone — she went in her sleep. But before Andre drives anywhere, Lena says wait. Mama left something. A message she recorded last week, on Lena's phone, for her brother first. And then his mother's voice fills the car: I'm proud of you. I know I didn't say it enough. I was proud of you every single day, even the days you thought I wasn't watching. Take care of your sister. Be good to each other. Andre sits in the car, keys in his hand, engine off, and says the only thing there is to say: please, play it again.
