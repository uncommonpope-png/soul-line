## Narration (~148 words)
Every Friday for two months, same order, same smile — Terrence walks up to Malik's food truck window and says, "Pay you Friday." But last Friday came and went, and the Friday before that. He already owes twenty-four dollars. Today he orders twelve more like the tab don't exist. Malik doesn't stop moving, doesn't shout — shouting loses the whole line. "That chicken cost me money. The oil cost me money. The gas cost me money. Every plate I give away is a plate I already paid for." "Supporting me is paying me," he says. "You want a favor, ask your mama. This window is a business." Terrence digs in his pocket and lays down a crumpled ten and two ones. Twelve dollars. Malik throws in a drink, on the house, and closes the tab for good. From now on, you pay when you order, like everybody else.
