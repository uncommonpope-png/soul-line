Jaylen is seventeen, standing in Ray's garage holding a wrench like it might bite him. His father sent him here to learn a trade. Jaylen wants to be a rapper. Ray knows it, Jaylen knows Ray knows it, and Ray doesn't laugh. He says a trade and a dream aren't enemies — music and cars are the same thing. Both are about listening for what's wrong and fixing it so it runs smooth. A man who can hear when a beat's off can hear when an engine's off. Same ear. So Jaylen gets under the car and hunts the rattle. A loose heat shield, two bolts, ten minutes — and when the engine runs clean, he grins like he just dropped a track. The old man gave the boy a wrench. The boy gave it back as a future.
