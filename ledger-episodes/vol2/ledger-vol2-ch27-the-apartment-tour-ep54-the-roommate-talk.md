## Narration (~150 words)
The apartment is hers, and Simone calls Jordan first — her friend since high school, sleeping on her cousin's couch three months too long. Jordan shows up with a tape measure and a notebook, checks the bedroom, then stands in the living room. "Alright. How much?" Five-fifty each, light bill and Wi-Fi split, groceries separate. Then Jordan asks the question most people ask too late: "If I'm late on rent one month — just short, not skipping — what happens? I don't want to find out in a fight." Simone respects that question. "Tell me before the first, not after. I cover it, you pay me back by the fifteenth, no extra charge, no attitude. But you don't go quiet on me. Quiet is what kills roommates." Jordan writes it down, then sends two-seventy-five on the spot — half her half — before Simone can ask. Deposit on the deposit.
