# Ledger Episodes — Index & Stitch Plan

Stick-figure episode scripts for the Ledger movie, written for Soul to animate. One file per conversation. **Episode order = book order.**

**Sources:**
- Vol 1: `book-13-the-ledger-script-rewritten.txt` — the Vol 1 rewrite (DRAFT script, 10 chapters, 34,293 words; NOT re-rendered as audio). 62 conversations, 62 FINAL SCORE blocks.
- Vol 2: `ledger-volume-2-combined-script.txt` — THE LEDGER: VOLUME TWO (27 chapters, 24,840 words; finished audio 3:16:51 exists, untouched by this job). 54 conversations.
- Merge notes: `ledger-merge/MERGE-NOTES.md`. Character system: `characters.md` in this folder (copied from Betty's stick-figure character lock).

**Totals: 116 episodes — Vol 1: 62, Vol 2: 54. Narration 16,420 words ≈ 117 minutes** (Vol 1: 8,672 words ≈ 62 min; Vol 2: 7,748 words ≈ 55 min), before ticker reads, title cards, and pauses. With those, the full movie runs roughly **2 hours 15 minutes**.

## Rules every episode follows

- **Narration 130–150 words (~60–90 seconds), hook in the first 3 seconds, no intro.** Adapted in our own words — never read the book aloud verbatim. These are our own PLT books, so adapting is allowed.
- **The ticker STAYS.** Every episode ends with the book's "P. L. T." score readout, copied verbatim, read aloud after the conversation. Never soften, reword, or drop it — it is the best part of the Ledger.
- **Animation is ten-second clothed clips stitched together** — 5–8 beats per episode, ~10 seconds each.
- **3–5 big on-screen labels** per episode (short phrases, all caps).
- **Characters (see `characters.md`):** Host ("YOU" — pink bow, pink PLT book, pink "222" badge, pink shoes) is the speaker who makes the mistake / learns. Profit is all-gold, Love is all-red, Tax is all-slate-grey, flat mouth, never smiles, zero pink — each enters ONLY when the narration names a gain, a feeling kept, or a cost. Other speakers are plain black figures. **Change ONLY the outfit layer** — never skeleton, head, line weight, or the PLT book + 222 badge. Rotate outfits; never repeat the same outfit in two consecutive episodes in the same chapter. Every figure wears our clothes, our style — Betty Plush fashion, branding visible.
- **Closing takeaway** is the book's own closing/takeaway line, verbatim, + the CTA: "The Ledger — free on the Soul Economy."

## Notes — read before animating

1. **20 derived titles (Vol 2, chapters 11–20).** The book gives these conversations NO titles — each chapter is two conversations split by a lone `---` divider, with a "The lesson:" line per conversation. Their episode titles were derived from the lesson line and are flagged `(production title — derived)` in the table and in each file. They are production labels, not book titles. Chapters 11–20 are the Vol 3 rooms (Airport, Diner at 2AM, Church, Bail Bondsman, Open House, The DM, Reunion, Mechanic, Graduation, Last Phone Call).
2. **2 conversations have no scores, by design (Vol 2).** Ep 16, The Debt (Ch 8, The Funeral), and Ep 39 (Ch 20, The Last Phone Call) — the book gives no "P. L. T." scores and no "Final:" line for these two. Ep 16 ends on the closing line "Pay it late, but pay it. A debt doesn't die with the man." Ep 39: the book says the corner man puts no number on it — "some moves are too big for numbers, and the silence after them is the score." Do NOT invent scores. Read the closing line (ep 16) / hold the silence (ep 39) instead of a ticker. (This also resolves the merge-notes count: 52 of 54 Vol 2 conversations have a "Final:" line; these 2 are the exceptions.)
3. **7 NEW rooms (Vol 2, chapters 21–27).** The Corner Store, The Cookout, The Bus Stop, The Nail Salon, The Food Truck, The Little League Game, The Apartment Tour — NEW MATERIAL ADDED FOR LENGTH, written to expand Volume Two to 3+ hours. Each of these conversations scores ONE speaker (one score line + one Final line each).
4. **Host calls are judgment calls.** The Host is the speaker who learns / makes the mistake, judged from the conversation — usually the second speaker or the lower final score, but not always (sometimes the protagonist scores higher). If a Host pick looks wrong for the animation, swap it — the narration and ticker don't change.
5. **Vol 1, Ep 62 is the book's finale.** Its ticker carries the book's closing lines ("The Ledger never stops running." … "Craig Jones, P. L. T. Press"). Keep them — they are the sign-off.
6. **This job is scripts only.** It does NOT touch the older 2026-10-26 ledger-movie project (62 conversations, 46 video assemblies, 16 incomplete/failed — the failed ones are NEVER retried, the incomplete ones need Ms GCP's word). Do not redo, retry, or touch those assemblies. These episode scripts are a separate, new thing.
