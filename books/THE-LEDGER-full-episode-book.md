# THE LEDGER — The Full Episode Book (Volume One)

Every conversation in *The Ledger* (Craig Jones & Lakeema Jones, PLT Press), split into episode scripts:
who's in the room, the beats, the on-screen words, the score after, and the takeaway —
with the scoring written to live **inside** the videos (opening card, live scorebug, delta chips, final score card),
the pink book locked in every scene, and a draft explainer line in Pope's slot on each episode.

62 episodes · 10 chapters. Production order is chapter order. Volume Two's 54 conversations join this book when their scripts land in the soul-line repo.
